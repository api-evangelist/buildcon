# Buildcon

> Buildcon is a Belgrade-based construction and logistics digitalisation company that builds Buildx, a construction project management platform (scheduling, cost tracking, daily logs, installed-quantities tracking, reporting), plus Buildx Warehouse and Buildx Tracking. Backed by 500 Global. Buildcon publishes no public developer API, SDK, or API documentation surface as of this profile.

- [Blog](https://www.buildcontech.com/blog): Articles on construction and logistics software

## Products
- Buildx: Construction project management (scheduling, cost tracking, daily logs, installed quantities, reporting)
- Buildx Warehouse: Warehouse management
- Buildx Tracking: Real-time asset tracking
